The problem

Your Wi-Fi stutters even on fast internet

Apple Wireless Direct Link (AWDL) powers AirDrop and Handoff — and it periodically hijacks your Wi-Fi radio mid-game. The result is latency that looks fine for minutes, then spikes exactly when timing matters.

◍

Invisible interference

AWDL wakes up for nearby Apple devices without asking. Nothing in the menu bar tells you it is the reason your ping tripled.

◫

Spikes, not slowness

Speed tests pass. Downloads fly. But real-time games feel rubber-banded because a few packets arrive hundreds of milliseconds late.

⬢

Manual fixes don't stick

Terminal commands work until the next reboot or OS update. Ping Warden watches continuously and re-blocks AWDL the moment it returns.
