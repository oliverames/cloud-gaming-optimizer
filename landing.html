Why it exists

Running sudo ifconfig awdl0 down once is not enough. macOS brings AWDL back within seconds, and a timer-based script reacts after the interface is already active, so the channel switch still hits your connection. Ping looks fine for minutes, then spikes exactly when the timing matters.

What it does

A privileged helper waits for kernel route and interface events. When macOS tries to raise awdl0 while Ping Protection is on, the helper takes it back down and counts the intervention. The dashboard puts that count next to live latency, jitter, and probe failures so you can see what happened on your own network.

What pauses

AirDrop, AirPlay, and Handoff pause while protection is active. That tradeoff is the point. Turn protection off when you need them, or use the 10-minute pause from the menu bar, and Ping Warden restores AWDL when protection stops.

Setup

From checkout to flat ping in four steps.

The app and your key arrive together. Gumroad emails the receipt, the download, and the license key the moment the purchase clears.

  1. Buy, then download

    The signed DMG is attached to your purchase. Open it from your receipt or your Gumroad library and drag Ping Warden to Applications.

  2. Approve the helper

    Click Set Up and Turn On Protection, then approve Ping Warden in System Settings. Changing the AWDL interface needs this one-time elevated access.

  3. Enter your key

    Paste the key from your receipt under Settings → License. It verifies once with Gumroad, then stays good for 14 days without a network.

  4. Turn on protection

    Flip Ping Protection on from the menu bar or Control Center. Watch the intervention counter climb while your ping stays flat.

FAQ

Asked before you ask.

Is Ping Warden itself free?

Yes. The app and its source are MIT-licensed. You only pay to enable Ping Protection inside the prebuilt, signed app. Build from source and you can use everything free.

Where do I download the app?

Right here. The signed, notarized build is attached to your purchase, so it is in your receipt and in your Gumroad library any time you need it again.

Does the key work with the GitHub download?

Yes. Any official build accepts the key from this purchase. Buying here bundles the download, the key, and updates in one place, but the key itself works wherever you got the app.

How many Macs does one key cover?

The Macs you own, desktop and laptop, with no seat counting. Enter the same key under Settings → License on each one.

What happens if I am offline?

Nothing breaks. After the first verification your license stays valid for 14 days without network access. While the app runs it re-checks quietly about every 6 hours, and once at launch.

What does Ping Protection turn off?

AirDrop, AirPlay, Handoff, and other nearby-device features that depend on AWDL, for as long as protection is on. The 10-minute pause brings them back without losing your setting.

Is this a subscription?

No. $15 once, and every future update is included. There is no renewal, no tier, and no feature drip.

What if it does not fix my lag?

Then AWDL was not your bottleneck, and you should not pay for a fix you do not need. The purchase is covered by Gumroad's standard refund terms, and the free dashboard will still show you what your ping is actually doing.

What does the app send anywhere?

Latency history, protection state, settings, and intervention counts stay on your Mac. The app checks Gumroad for your key, checks the public appcast for updates, and asks GeForce NOW for its region list. Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on.
