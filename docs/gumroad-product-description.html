Ping Warden is an open-source macOS menu bar app that helps reduce interruptions from wireless sharing by blocking AWDL (Apple Wireless Direct Link) while you play. The source stays MIT and everything except enabling Ping Protection is free.

This purchase includes the signed, notarized app download plus a license that unlocks Ping Protection, the AWDL-blocking feature.

  • Signed updates via Sparkle. Upgrades from free versions show the license terms before installation.

How to activate

  1. Download the app from your Gumroad receipt or library
  2. Open Settings → License, enter your key, and click Verify
  3. Click Turn On Ping Protection in the welcome window, or Finish Setup in Settings → General. Approve the helper in System Settings when prompted
  4. Turn on Ping Protection from the menu bar or Control Center
