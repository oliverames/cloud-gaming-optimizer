## Flagged, not fixed (judgment calls)

1. **Helper TOCTOU** (`PingWardenMonitor.m` setAwdlEnabled): poller death
   between the `_threadRunning` check and pipe write can acknowledge a command
   nothing enforces, and `awdlEnabled` keeps reporting the stale atomic until
   process restart. Window is microscopic, subsequent commands fail loudly,
   and launchd restarts the daemon on demand. Closing it properly means a
   state-ack protocol or post-write flag verification; closing fd-on-exit was
   ruled out because it turns future writes into SIGPIPE kills.
2. **Event-driven intervention counts**: four independent 5 s XPC pollers
   could become notification-driven with a slow reconciliation fallback.
   Half-day refactor touching the Obj-C daemon; deferred pending appetite.
3. **Custom "treat as game" apps**: Game Mode never fires for fullscreen
   browsers playing web-based cloud gaming; needs a settings-surface design
   decision before implementation.
